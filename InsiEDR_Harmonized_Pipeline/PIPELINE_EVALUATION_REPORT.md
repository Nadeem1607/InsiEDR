## 4. Multi-Stage Correlation Analysis: How `SOCL4-61` Was Caught

The 4-stage kill-chain on `SOCL4-61` spanned **3.5 continuous hours** (`2026-10-12 09:00 to 12:30 UTC`). The multi-feed cross-domain fusion correlated the entire attack lifecycle:

1. **Stage 1 (09:00–09:45) - Credential Dumping**:
   * Captured by `lsass-monitor` and `short-term_edr_feature` (elevated handle queries and authentication rate surges).
2. **Stage 2 (09:45–10:30) - Lateral Movement & Reconnaissance**:
   * Captured by `short-term_edr_feature` (`unique_workstations_300s = 4`, multi-target cross-authentication).
3. **Stage 3 (10:30–11:30) - Data Staging & Canary Tampering**:
   * Captured by `file-integrity-monitor` and `decoy-monitor` (file entropy shifts and staging creation).
4. **Stage 4 (11:30–12:30) - Cloud Exfiltration**:
   * Captured by `http` and `network` (external upload bursts and outbound connection anomalies).

Because `SOCL4-61` accumulated active anomalous ticks across **all 4 tactical phases**, its persistence multiplier escalated its score to **3.7852**, completely separating it from benign background workstations that only produced brief, isolated spikes in a single collector.
